🌿 Exercises and Yoga Tips for a Healthy Mind 🤖

✨ Do regular stretches for flexibility. Try the Butterfly Pose 🦋.
🧘‍♀️ Practice yoga to improve strength and balance. Try Tree Pose 🌳 for focus.
🌬️ Take time for deep breathing exercises. Try Pranayama 🫁 for relaxation.
💪 Incorporate strength training for muscle tone. Squats & Planks work wonders! 🏋️‍♂️
🧘‍♂️ Practice mindfulness with yoga for mental clarity. Try Shavasana (Corpse Pose) 💤.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our AI-powered mental health chatbot.

How does the AI chatbot support mental health?

Our AI-powered chatbot provides real-time emotional support, mood tracking, and personalized coping strategies based on your interactions.

Is my conversation with the chatbot confidential?

Yes! We use end-to-end encryption and anonymization techniques to ensure complete privacy and confidentiality of your conversations.

Can the chatbot diagnose mental health conditions?

No, the chatbot is designed to provide support and self-care strategies, but it does not replace professional diagnosis or therapy.

How does the chatbot track my mood and emotions?

Using sentiment analysis, the chatbot detects emotional patterns over time and provides tailored wellness insights based on your conversations.

Is the chatbot suitable for teenagers and students?

Yes, the chatbot is designed to be youth-friendly, offering supportive and age-appropriate guidance for students and teenagers.

What should I do if I need immediate help or feel unsafe?

If you're in crisis or need urgent support, we strongly encourage you to reach out to a mental health helpline or emergency services in your area. The chatbot also provides quick links to professional resources.

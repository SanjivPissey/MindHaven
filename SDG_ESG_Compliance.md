# APPENDIX B: SDG / ESG COMPLIANCE

## Compliance with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

**Identified SDG along with target and Indicator:**  
**SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-Being**  
*   **Target 3.4:** By 2030, reduce by one third premature mortality from non-communicable diseases through prevention and treatment and promote mental health and well-being.
*   **Indicator 3.4.2:** Suicide mortality rate.

**Contribution of the Project towards the identified SDG:**
*   Promotes mental health and well-being by providing accessible, 24/7 AI-driven emotional support to individuals facing stress, anxiety, or depression.
*   Acts as an immediate "First Aid" intervention tool, reducing the barrier to entry and the stigma often associated with seeking initial mental health assistance.
*   Actively monitors user inputs for signs of severe distress and uses a specialized **Crisis Detection Module** to provide immediate comforting responses while escalating the case to human professionals.
*   Continuously tracks user emotional states through automated **Mood and Stress Detection**, allowing for long-term emotional well-being analysis.
*   Bridges the gap between patients and mental health professionals through the **Psychologist Dashboard**, enabling data-driven therapy and timely interventions.
*   Provides accessible self-help resources (e.g., yoga, music therapy links) within the platform to encourage proactive mental health management.
*   Supports centralized healthcare management by maintaining secure, digital records of patient chat histories and mood trends for professional review.
